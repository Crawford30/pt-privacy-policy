In short

The Present Tense DVDs apps for Android and iPhone let approved members listen to and watch the ministry's DVDs. We keep only what's needed to run your account and the app. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

You can delete your account and its data at any time from the app: Settings → Delete account. See how to delete your account.

What we keep

Your account

  • Email address and an account ID. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Access settings made by the administrators: whether you're verified, your region, which DVD lists you can open, how many devices you may use, and your personal access code.

Your devices

  • For each phone signed in to your account: a random ID made by the app (not your phone's serial number or advertising ID), the phone model and system version, and when it was last used. This is how the device limit works.

What you listen to and watch

  • For each DVD you play: its title and date, how many times you've played it, how long you've spent playing it, how far you got, whether you finished it, when you last played it, and whether that was on Android or iPhone.

Crash reports (Android)

  • If the Android app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics receives a report: the phone model, system version, app version and technical details of the crash. It doesn't include your email or what you were listening to.

Kept only on your phone

  • DVDs you download, where you left off in each DVD, and your settings (theme, text size, playback speed). These stay on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Why we keep it

  • To sign you in and give you the DVDs your account is approved for.
  • To limit how many phones can use one account, so accounts aren't shared.
  • To let you carry on from where you stopped, on any of your phones.
  • To let the ministry's administrators see which DVDs are being listened to and watched, so they can better serve members.
  • To find and fix crashes.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Who can see it

  • You can see your account, your devices (if the administrators have turned that on) and your own progress in the app.
  • The ministry's administrators can see account details, access settings and devices, so they can approve accounts and help you. A small number of senior administrators can also see listening history.
  • Nobody else, apart from the service providers below, who store the data for us.

Services we use

These companies handle data under their own privacy policies and only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Your account information and listening history are kept until you delete your account, or until the administrators remove it. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

Delete your account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete.

This straight away deletes your login, your email address, your access settings and access code, your devices and your listening history, and signs you out on every phone. It can't be undone. To use the app again you'd need to register and be approved again.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, email joelcrawford30@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, saying you'd like your account deleted. We'll delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

Downloads and settings stored on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep information about them. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored with Google Firebase, where database rules let each person reach only their own information, and administrators only what their role allows. Access codes are personal and can't be read back by the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions about your data, or requests to see or delete it: email joelcrawford30@gmail.com.
